# Product

## Register

product

## Users

PSG Hub serves collision-repair shop owners, shop marketing leads, PSG account managers, PSG production and billing operators, strategy staff, and superadmins. Review-workspace users are usually non-technical reviewers checking PSG-prepared content before it is sent to customers or published.

## Product Purpose

PSG Hub is the unified operating platform for Body Shop Marketer and PSG internal work. It brings customer marketing visibility, content approvals, invoicing, production workflows, reporting, and internal market intelligence into one controlled system. Success means shop customers can understand and approve PSG work quickly, while PSG operators can manage the work without fragmented tools.

## Brand Personality

Understated, expert, accountable. The product should feel calm and reliable for business owners, with enough density for PSG operators who use it repeatedly. It should explain actions plainly and avoid decorative interface choices that distract from the work.

## Anti-references

Do not make PSG Hub feel like a generic marketing landing page, a playful consumer app, or a flashy artificial-intelligence demo. Avoid oversized hero layouts inside task screens, decorative gradients, vague button labels, unclear empty states, and invented controls where standard product UI patterns work better.

## Design Principles

1. Keep the task visible: customers and operators should always know what they are reviewing, changing, or approving.
2. Use plain language: labels and messages should explain the next action without technical knowledge.
3. Preserve trust: customer-facing and approval screens should make status, ownership, and consequences clear.
4. Prefer focused density: internal tools should be compact enough for repeated work without hiding important context.
5. Respect PSG's brand system: use the established restrained palette, typography, and square-leaning geometry.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Customer-facing and review surfaces should meet WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-accessibility expectations. Motion should be minimal and respect reduced-motion preferences. Approval workflows must not rely on color alone to communicate status or decisions.

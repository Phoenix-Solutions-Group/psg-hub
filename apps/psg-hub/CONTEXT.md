# PSG Hub

PSG Hub combines customer and internal collision-repair workflows. These terms keep repair-claim intelligence separate from the market for insurance purchased by repair shops.

## Collision insurance intelligence

**Repair-claim carrier**:
The insurer responsible for a vehicle owner's repair claim and recorded on a repair order.
_Avoid_: Insurance company, shop insurer

**Body-shop coverage provider**:
An insurer offering commercial protection to a collision-repair business itself.
_Avoid_: Repair-claim carrier

**Appetite evidence**:
Current first-party carrier material that explicitly identifies body, paint, or collision shops as a target business class.
_Avoid_: Availability, eligibility, recommendation

**State authorization**:
Regulatory evidence that a legal underwriting entity may offer the relevant insurance product in a specific state.
_Avoid_: National availability

**Policy observation**:
Evidence from a current PSG customer policy or broker confirmation that coverage was actually placed.
_Avoid_: Appetite, quote

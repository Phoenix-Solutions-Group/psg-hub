Filter model

  • Date range defaults to last 90 days.
  • Shop filter defaults to the signed-in shop.
  • Role filter supports estimator, body technician, painter, and customer service representative when data supports it.
  • Alert state supports all, open, assigned, resolved, below-threshold, negative comment, and public review issue.

Drill-down paths

  • Summary cards open filtered source surveys.
  • Funnel steps open the matching sent, returned, recommended, low-score, or review records.
  • Trend points open that month’s source surveys.
  • Role rows open attributed jobs and surveys only when join confidence is confirmed.

UX assumptions to resolve

  • Ada must confirm repair-order and employee role joins before role attribution becomes customer-visible.
  • PMO must confirm whether follow-up status exists now or starts as read-only alert state.
  • Vera must confirm public-review sentiment is safe for version-one customer visibility.
  • Survey sub-score labels must be verified against customer-facing survey wording.
